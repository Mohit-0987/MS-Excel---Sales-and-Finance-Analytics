# Excel Sales & Finance Analytics — Business Case Study

This project demonstrates how Excel-based sales and financial analytics can support business decisions and growth using a case study built around **AtliQ Hardware**. The objective was to transform raw operational data into meaningful insights that improve strategy, performance tracking, and financial planning.

---

## Project Overview

This project combines structured data analysis with business understanding. Raw datasets were transformed into analytical models and interactive reports to highlight how data-driven decision-making impacts both sales operations and financial outcomes.

---

## Methodology

### 1. Data Extraction & Transformation (ETL)
**Tool:** Power Query (Excel)

Data was collected from multiple sources, cleaned, standardized, and transformed into analysis-ready formats. This ensured consistency and accuracy across datasets, reducing reporting errors.

**Outcome:** Reliable, high-quality data foundation for modeling and analysis.

---

### 2. Data Modeling
**Tool:** Power Pivot (Excel)

Relationships were built between fact and dimension tables to create a scalable data model. This enabled multi-dimensional analysis across time, products, and markets.

**Outcome:** Dynamic data model supporting flexible reporting.

---

### 3. Sales Analytics

**Focus Areas:**
- Customer performance analysis  
- Purchase behavior patterns  
- Market performance vs targets  

**Outcome:** Actionable insights to evaluate sales efficiency and guide strategic improvements.

---

### 4. Finance Analytics

**Focus Areas:**
- Profit & Loss analysis across markets  
- Revenue vs cost structure evaluation  
- Financial performance tracking  

**Outcome:** Better understanding of profitability drivers and budgeting decisions.

---

### 5. Visualization & Reporting
**Tool:** Excel Pivot Tables & Dashboards

Reports were designed to be structured, intuitive, and decision-focused, allowing business users to interpret insights quickly.

**Outcome:** Clear and informative visual reporting.

---

## Key Learnings

- Strong ETL processes improve data reliability  
- Structured data models enable deeper analysis  
- Time-based reporting enhances trend visibility  
- Sales analytics reveals customer and market opportunities  
- Financial analytics clarifies profitability drivers  
- Effective visualization improves decision-making
